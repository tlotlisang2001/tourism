About Lesotho

Discover the beauty, culture, history and adventures of the Kingdom in the Sky.

Who We Are

Explore Lesotho is a tourism website created to showcase the beauty, culture, history and tourist attractions of Lesotho. It helps visitors discover interesting places to visit and activities to enjoy.

Our goal is to encourage people to explore Lesotho and appreciate its mountains, waterfalls, wildlife and Basotho cultural heritage.

A video Basotho men singing Mokorotlo

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote tourism in Lesotho by providing useful information about tourist attractions, cultural heritage and outdoor activities.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a useful online platform that inspires people to visit Lesotho and experience its natural beauty and culture.

Why Visit Lesotho?

Lesotho offers beautiful mountain scenery, fresh air, cultural experiences and exciting outdoor adventures. It is a wonderful destination for visitors who want to discover something unique.
